## 1. How does the `Transaction` / `GlobalQueue` scheduler (`core/scheduler.ts`) implement automatic transitions, microtask batching, `flush()`, `onFlush()`, and `onSettled()` — specifically how are in-flight async changes held/staged (`joinFuture`, `passTx`, `resolveTx`, `staleReader`, `flushTransaction`, `dirtyQueue`) so the UI keeps showing consistent previous state instead of tearing?

### Takeaway

`@solidjs/signals` 2.0.3 batches writes onto a microtask `flush()` that runs a pure `dirtyQueue` heap phase followed by a `settle()` seam: if any pass joined a `Transaction` (via an async read reaching an observer, touching a held node, or an active action), all `pendingNodes` and stashed effect queues park in that transaction until `blocked(t)` becomes false, while outside render effects are served committed values and queued as `staleReader` entries for re-derivation after landing.

## 2. How do `isPending()` and `latest()` (`core/verdict.ts`) inspect in-flight/staged values vs committed values, and how do `refresh()` and `affects()` (`affects.ts`) trigger quiet vs pending revalidation?

### Takeaway

`isPending(fn)` and `latest(fn)` mark the reading pass as a verdict reader (`CONFIG_VERDICT`, routed into the holder's `verdictLane(t)`) and swap the global `verdict` read hook to `verdictValue`, allowing `latest` to read staged/pending proposals (`_pendingValue`, `latestOf`, `aheadError`) and `isPending` to probe for non-quiet flights, non-netting staged changes, or `affects()` marks without tearing or blocking the main frame. `refresh()` issues a quiet re-ask (`_reask`) that keeps `isPending()` false by default, whereas `affects()` attaches explicit pending marks (`_marks` / `ambient`) that make `isPending()` return true until the transaction or flight settles.

## 3.
