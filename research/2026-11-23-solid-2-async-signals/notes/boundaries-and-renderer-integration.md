## 1. How do `createLoadingBoundary`, `createErrorBoundary` (`packages/signals/src/boundaries.ts`), and `createRevealOrder` (`packages/signals/src/reveal.ts`) catch `NotReadyError` / runtime errors, decide when to show a fallback vs hold previous UI (including `<Loading on={...}>` re-arming, `enforceLoadingBoundary`, and automatic error healing), and coordinate reveal ordering?

### Takeaway

Boundaries catch status both via `GlobalQueue.notify` (`catchHook` -> `catchStatus`) and directly when `output` inspects `tree._statusFlags` or catches `NotReadyError`/errors from `read(tree)`. A loading boundary shows its fallback only while uninitialized (`!_initialized`), re-armed (`_armed`), or behind an error fallback, otherwise forwarding pending reads to hold the frame on a transaction; error boundaries track the throwing source and heal automatically via `catchStatus(..., 0)` / `prune()` once the source settles, while `createRevealOrder` gates direct child `<Loading>` boundaries until their policy (`sequential`, `together`, `natural`) releases them.
